# Privacy Policy (Callora)

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Callora ("the app") is a call continuity assistant for Android users in
Bangladesh, developed by Shohan Khan (helloiamshohan@gmail.com).

## What the app stores

- SIM subscription details exposed to apps holding READ_PHONE_STATE
  (subscription handle, display/carrier name, slot, state, MCC-MNC).
- Forwarding destination numbers you enter, plus profiles, schedules and
  forwarding activity history.
- App preferences such as language and theme.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet permission and sends nothing to any server.
- It does not access contacts, call logs, SMS, location, microphone or camera.
- It does not include advertising, analytics or third-party trackers.
- It does not store ICCID, IMSI or your line phone number.

## Where data lives

All data stays in app-private storage on your device and in your own Android
backup if you enable system backup. You can erase it at any time from
Settings → Privacy and data → Clear all stored configuration. Uninstalling the
app removes the same data.

## Permissions

See [docs/PERMISSIONS.md](docs/PERMISSIONS.md) for the exact permission list,
when each is requested, and what happens if you deny one.

## Carrier commands

Callora can open the system dialer with standard GSM forwarding codes that you
explicitly confirm. Your mobile operator processes those commands under its own
terms and tariffs; Callora cannot see or control what the operator does with the
call once forwarded.
